Why CyberFenix

Tools that don't ask you to trade away your privacy

Most tools like these want OAuth access to your account, then do the work on their own servers. Ours don't work that way — and can't.

Your data stays local

Reading, rendering, archiving and every list you build happen inside your own browser. Nothing of yours is ever uploaded, anywhere.

No account access

No OAuth, no Google Cloud project, no consent screen. Our extensions work inside the browser session you already have — or, in LocalTube's case, with no sign-in at all.

Open source

The whole extension is MIT-licensed and published on GitHub. Don't take our word for the privacy claims — read the code.

Useful before you pay

LocalTube is free outright. Gmail Bulk Extractor gives every install 7 days of Pro, then stays free and usable — the upgrade is for volume, not basic function.

Questions

Common questions

Do you have access to my emails?

No. Our extensions run entirely in your browser and read mail through the Gmail session you are already signed into. There is no server of ours in the path, no OAuth token, and no copy of your mail anywhere but your own machine.

Do I need a CyberFenix account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. If you choose to upgrade to Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, and the only thing stored is whether your licence is active.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, and Chromium-based browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store — Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi. Firefox and Safari are not supported today.

Is it really free?

LocalTube is free outright — there is no paid tier and nothing to unlock. Gmail Bulk Extractor is free with a limit: every install starts with 7 days of full Pro, after which the core actions stay free up to 100 emails a week, and Pro lifts the limit and unlocks ZIP export, Merge, and custom file naming.

Can I read the source?

Yes — both Gmail Bulk Extractor and LocalTube are on GitHub under the MIT licence, including the exact code that ships to the Web Store.
